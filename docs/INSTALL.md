# Installing Librarium

Librarium is a Mac app for notes and a reading library. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or
later) and macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer.

## Install

1. Download **[Librarium.dmg](https://github.com/PartlyWhole/Librarium/releases/latest/download/Librarium.dmg)**.
2. Open it, and drag **Librarium** onto **Applications**.
3. Open Librarium from Applications. The first time, macOS stops it with *“Librarium” Not
   Opened: Apple could not verify “Librarium” is free of malware…*. Click **Done**. This
   happens because Librarium isn't registered with Apple (that costs a yearly fee), not because
   something is wrong with it.
4. Open **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security**, scroll down to *“Librarium” was blocked to
   protect your Mac*, and click **Open Anyway**. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then
   click **Open Anyway** once more.

That's needed once. From then on Librarium opens like any other app.

## First start

Librarium asks for a folder to keep your library in: your notes, items and captures, as
ordinary files you can back up or open elsewhere. A new, empty folder is best, e.g. a folder
named *Librarium* in your home folder.

If you choose a folder inside Documents, Desktop or Downloads, macOS asks whether Librarium may
use it: click **Allow**. (It may ask again after an update; click Allow again. Outside those
three folders it never asks.)

## Updates

Librarium looks for a new version once a day. When there is one it says *Librarium 0.2.0 is
available*. Click **Update…**, then **Install**. When it's ready it offers **Restart now**; your
work is saved first. If you choose **Later**, the new version starts the next time you open
Librarium.

You can also look at any time: **Librarium ▸ Check for Updates…**

## Removing it

Drag Librarium from Applications to the Bin. Your library folder stays where it is, untouched.

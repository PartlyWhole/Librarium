# 0034. Tabs, as in Obsidian

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03

## Context and problem

The user asked for tabs like Obsidian's: several notes, items and folders open at once, each
keeping its place.

## Decision

- **The router holds tabs.** Each tab has its own history, so back and forward work in it.
  - `shell.router.current` is the active tab's route, so features keep calling
    `router.go(...)`.
  - `go(..., { newTab, background })` and `openRecord(id, params, { newTab })` open in a new
    tab.
  - The route and the active tab change together (in one batch), so nothing sees a route in
    the wrong tab.
- **Pages stay alive per tab.** Each tab renders into its own host, hidden while another tab
  shows. A document, a PDF's place or an editor's state survives a switch, and so does the
  page's scroll.
  - A tab's page is rendered again only when that tab goes somewhere else.
  - Closing a tab disposes its page.
  - Each tab keeps its own title, header buttons and "where new things go" (`shell.here`).
  - Pages that listen on the whole window ignore keys while their tab is hidden.
- **The tab bar** sits above the page header, following the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern.
  - **Mouse:** a click shows a tab, a middle-click or its × closes it, and a drag along the bar
    moves it. Its context menu offers Close, Close other tabs, New tab and moving left or right.
  - **Keyboard:** the arrows move between tabs, and Delete closes one.
  - **The ×** is for the pointer only (nothing interactive inside a tab); the keyboard has ⌘W
    and Delete.
- **Shortcuts:**
  - ⌘T opens a new tab, and ⌘W closes the tab. Closing the last tab leaves a new one.
  - ⇧⌘T reopens the last closed tab, with its history.
  - ⌃Tab and ⌃⇧Tab (also ⇧⌘] and ⇧⌘[) switch tabs; ⌘1–⌘8 pick a tab, and ⌘9 the last.
  - ⇧⌘W closes the window. The native "Close Window" item, which took ⌘W, is gone.
  - Today moves from ⌘T to ⇧⌘D.
- **Opening in a new tab:**
  - "Open in new tab" is in records' and folders' menus.
  - A middle-click works on sidebar rows and page entries, and on links in notes.
  - ⌘-click on a link in a note also opens it in a new tab, and ⌘↩ does in a page's list.
- **The "New tab" page** lists where to go (Open…, each page) and what was opened recently.
- **The tabs are kept between runs** in the per-device preference `ui.tabs` (routes and
  histories) and restored when the library opens.

## Addendum (R-023): no duplicate tabs

Two tabs never show the same place: a record (by its ID, whatever the place in it) or a page
(by its params). Going to a place open in another tab shows that tab, at the place asked for;
"in a new tab", reopening and restoring follow the same rule (`placeOf` in `router.ts`).

## Consequences

- **Every open tab holds its page in memory.** That is fine for the handful of tabs people
  keep, and closing a tab frees it.
- **Restored routes may point at records that are gone;** those pages say so, as before.
